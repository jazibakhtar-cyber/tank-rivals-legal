MOON GAMES · TANK RIVALS

Privacy Policy

Effective: 12 November 2026 · Last updated: 12 November 2026

Plain-language summary. Tank Rivals is an offline game with local progress. It does not require a player account, multiplayer connection or cloud save. The game uses Google AdMob to display ads, and Google’s advertising technology processes the data described below.

1. Who we are

Tank Rivals (package com.moongames.tankrivals) is published by Moon Games (“Moon Games”, “we”, “us” or “our”). Questions about this policy can be sent to moongames303@gmail.com.

2. Game data stored on your device

Tank Rivals stores gameplay information such as progress, settings, virtual coins, unlocked content, selected equipment and temporary tank-rental status locally on your device. Moon Games does not operate a player-account database or cloud-save service for this version. Removing the app or clearing its storage may permanently remove this local progress.

3. Advertising and data handled by Google

The game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) for app-open, menu banner, interstitial and optional rewarded advertisements. Rewarded ads are initiated by the player in exchange for the clearly stated in-game reward.

Google states that the Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share:

  • Approximate location, inferred from the device’s IP address;
  • App interactions, such as app launches, taps, ad impressions and video views;
  • Diagnostics, such as SDK or app performance, launch time, hang rate and energy use; and
  • Device or other identifiers, including the Android advertising ID, app set ID and related identifiers where available.

This information may be used for advertising or marketing, analytics, and fraud prevention, security and compliance. Google reports that data collected by the Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. Google acts under its own privacy terms; see Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information for advertising.

4. Consent and advertising choices

Where required, Tank Rivals uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to request or refresh advertising consent and to display privacy choices. Depending on your region and choice, Google may provide personalized or non-personalized/limited ads. You can also reset or delete the Android advertising ID through your device’s privacy settings. Refusing personalized advertising does not necessarily remove all advertising or all processing needed to deliver, measure and protect ads.

5. What Moon Games does not collect directly

This version does not ask Moon Games for your name, account password, contacts, messages, photos, videos, audio recordings, precise GPS location, health information, payment-card information or browsing history. The game has no player chat, account registration, multiplayer service or in-app purchase system.

6. Children

Tank Rivals is not designed to request personal information from children. A parent or guardian should supervise a minor’s device and advertising choices where required by local law. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to Moon Games, contact us so we can investigate.

7. Retention and deletion

Local gameplay data remains on the device until the app’s storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Advertising data is handled according to Google’s retention controls and policies. Moon Games cannot identify or delete data held solely by Google without sufficient information, but privacy questions may be sent to us and advertising identifiers can be reset or deleted in Android settings.

8. International processing and security

Google may process advertising information in countries other than your own under its applicable safeguards. Moon Games uses the SDK’s supported privacy and encrypted-transport mechanisms, but no electronic system can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to information, access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or withdrawal of consent. You may contact Moon Games at the address below. You may also use the in-app Google privacy form when it is required and available in your region.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game, advertising SDKs, legal requirements or data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when a material update is published.
